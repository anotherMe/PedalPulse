# Phase 3, Plan 03: Master volume in the engine, and VO2 at full volume never clips

## Phase Goal

**As a** rider, **I want to** pick my effort zone and set the volume, **so that** the music's energy matches my interval without clipping.

This plan adds the engine half of master volume end to end. The real Python client sends `/pp/volume`, sclang validates it, and a smoothed gain inside the master synth, before the limiter, makes the music quieter or louder. It then proves the phase's safety promise: with VO2's EPIC and the volume at maximum, the output still never exceeds the limiter ceiling. Plan 04 puts the volume slider in the window on top of this client.

<objective>
Add the `/pp/volume <float 0-100>` control: `config.OSC_VOLUME_ADDRESS`, `OscClient.send_volume` plus a `--volume` CLI option, and in `supercollider/main.scd` a volume control bus, a volume taper function and a `volumeBus` argument on `\ppMaster` that applies a 0.2 s lagged gain before `Limiter.ar`. Extend the headless smoke test so loudness change, silence, clamp, validation, de-zippering, re-evaluation and the max-EPIC-max-volume ceiling are each checked through the real engine.

Purpose: GUI-03 (adjust master volume; engine half) and ROADMAP Phase 3 success criterion 4 (loudness changes smoothly; VO2 at maximum volume never clips).
Output: updated `python_app/config.py`, `python_app/osc_client.py`, `python_app/tests/test_osc_client.py`, `supercollider/main.scd`, `supercollider/smoke_test.scd`.

Working decisions this plan implements (RESEARCH "Gray Areas" defaults adopted as working decisions; no CONTEXT.md exists for Phase 3):
- Wire contract `/pp/volume <float>` on sclang's language port, loopback-only, engine clamps to 0-100 (Phase 1 D-19 pre-named it; rated costly, see Task 1).
- Volume lives inside `\ppMaster`, multiplied in next to `preGain` and before `Limiter.ar`. It is never a separate synth and never applied after the limiter (RESEARCH Pattern 5, Pitfall 1). Its lag is 0.2 s, shorter than EPIC's 1.5 s: it removes zipper steps without becoming a musical fade.
- Default volume 80 on a fresh boot; re-evaluation keeps the last value.
- **Planner deviation from RESEARCH (Claude's discretion, reversible):** RESEARCH maps 0-100 linearly to gain. This plan uses a squared taper, gain = (v / 100) squared: 100 → 1.0 (unity, never a boost), 80 → 0.64 (about −3.9 dB), 50 → 0.25 (about −12 dB). With a linear map the top half of the slider changes loudness by only about 6 dB while the bottom tenth spans from silence to −20 dB. Squaring is the standard cheap approximation of an audio taper, so equal slider moves feel like roughly equal loudness changes. It is one engine function, `~ppVolumeAmp`. Going back to linear means editing that one line, and the smoke checks derive their expected values from it. The Plan 04 listening check asks whether the slider feels even.
</objective>
