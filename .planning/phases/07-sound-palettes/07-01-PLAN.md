# Phase 7, Plan 01: Pick a palette and the whole band changes sound together; supersaw is the first one

## Phase Goal

**As a** rider, **I want to** have the engine change its whole sound set every few minutes, or keep one I like, **so that** a long ride never sounds the same and I can name what I want more or less of.

This plan is the tracer for the phase. Task 1 wires the palette through the core: the memoized per-bar harmony entry now also says which palette the bar uses, every one of the seven layers (the drums too) reads it, and the post window names every palette change. It is proven with a temporary palette that replaces all seven voices: they all switch on the same bar. Task 2 builds the measuring tool every palette has to pass before it is frozen (loudness against classic, headroom at VO2, CPU, extreme notes), wires it into the library-only gate, and ships the first real palette, `supersaw`, tuned against it.

<objective>
Make the palette per-bar state shared by all seven layers (RESEARCH Pattern 1), announce every palette change in the post window (D-09), build the headless palette meter and its gate (D-13, D-14, D-15 headless part), and ship `supersaw`, measured before its first commit.

Purpose: PAL-01 (first named palette), PAL-03 (a named pick takes over from the next bar, D-11), PAL-04 (loudness matched against classic, never above the limiter ceiling).
Output: modified harmony.scd, render.scd, library_test.scd, check.sh; new palette_meter.scd, scripts/palette_level.sh and library/palettes/supersaw.scd. No Python change.

Planner decisions recorded here (yolo mode; the rider reviews them at the end-of-phase UAT, Plan 06):
- Stem `supersaw` instead of D-01's working name `anthem` (RESEARCH Pitfall 11): `anthem` is already a progression, and "palette anthem" next to "progression anthem" would make plain-language feedback ambiguous. D-01 lets the planner adjust stems.
- The level metric (D-13, planner's choice): post-FX RMS (the ~ppFxBus, after the shared EPIC effects and before the master volume and limiter) averaged over exactly 4 bars, after 2 bars of settling, at 180 BPM with progression `anthem` and melody `classic_lead_loud` (so the palette's bass, pad and lead are all heard), EPIC 50. Gate: the gap to classic at most 1.5 dB; authoring target 0.75 dB so the gate holds at other EPIC levels (RESEARCH Pitfall 2). Headroom gate at EPIC 100 and volume 100: post-FX peak at most 0.80 (RESEARCH A3) and output peak at most 0.892 (limiter ceiling plus tolerance) and above 0.1 (real audio). CPU: mean of `s.avgCPU` samples taken every 0.25 s during the VO2 window; `CHECK WARN` above 12%, failure at 25% (D-14). Nodes: at most 40 synths during the VO2 window (RESEARCH Pitfall 10). Extreme notes (RESEARCH Open Question 6, taken into scope): every pitched voice of a non-classic palette is spawned alone at MIDI -12 and 127 (the WR-01 note bounds Plan 02 adds) and must stay finite with a peak below 4.0.
- The level measurement lives in its own isolated-server script (`supercollider/palette_meter.scd`, port 57142) that `scripts/check.sh --library-only` runs after library_test.scd, instead of inside library_test.scd (RESEARCH "Alternatives Considered"). To the rider it is the same single command; it keeps one definition of the metric for the gate and for authoring (`scripts/palette_level.sh <name>`, about 1 minute), and it leaves the library check's own watchdog alone.
- A pad held across a next-bar manual switch (CONTEXT discretion): the old palette's pad keeps ringing to its own release and the new palette's pad enters at the next chord change. No core change, no gap (the old pad is still sounding), no double attack (D-10).
</objective>
