# Phase 6, Plan 01: Pick anthem and the whole band moves through Am F C G together

## Phase Goal

**As a** rider, **I want to** hear the music move through chord progressions that bass, arp and pad follow together, **so that** it stops sounding like the same few notes for an hour.

This plan is the tracer for the phase. It wires one progression, `anthem`, through every layer the phase touches: a chord-data library file, the validator, a new core harmony module (one memoized chord schedule per run that every pitched layer pulls), the renderer (bass and pad from the core, arp and lead mapped onto the chord), and `main.scd`. The proof is a real recording of anthem with the lead playing: every pitched note sits in its own bar's chord, while the classic parity check stays at 0 mismatches. Task 2 proves a mid-cycle pick lands on the cycle boundary.

<objective>
Make `/pp/progression anthem` play Am F C G with bass, pad, arp and lead on the same chord every bar, switching only at cycle boundaries, while classic stays the golden note for note.

Purpose: HARM-01 (shared chord, no clashes), HARM-03 (nearest pad voicing, held across the chord), HARM-04 (named pick at the end of the cycle). Implements D-01, D-02, D-03 (arp and lead follow the chord, notes only), D-05 (chords carry their real pitch classes, scale exposed), D-06 (per-file pace), D-07 (key of A from the core), D-11 (post-window line), D-12, D-13 (bass), D-14 (pad).
Output: new `supercollider/core/harmony.scd` and `supercollider/library/progressions/anthem.scd`; modified validate, render, loader, main, library_test and smoke_test.

Decisions this plan records (CONTEXT "Claude's Discretion" and RESEARCH open questions, resolved here):
- Bass and pad are core default generators that read the plan chord. A progression file is chord data; a file may still define `bass`/`pad` generators to override the core's part. A file without `chords` (only `classic.scd` today) keeps today's rule: a missing role plays classic's generator.
- The `classic` progression is "Am every bar": the core gives it an implicit Am chord with `extraPcs: [5]`, because the frozen classic pad plays a passing F on odd bars. That allowance exists only for this implicit chord; every chord-data file is checked strictly (RESEARCH Pitfall 3, Open Question 2).
- Arp and lead mapping (D-01/D-02) uses the chord-tone ladder: a note is located on the Am ladder (A C E in every octave, nearest, ties to the lower note) and the same ladder position is read off the current chord's root, third and fifth, with the root octave chosen so the triad's mean is nearest Am's (60.333). Passing tones therefore become chord tones. Under the classic progression the rows are left untouched (byte-identical), so test fixtures and the golden keep their notes.
- A melody module may declare `ownHarmony: true`; then its own generator's rows are not mapped (Phase 8 melodies read `plan[\chord]` themselves). Rows that came from the classic fallback are always mapped (RESEARCH Open Question 1). This is a planner decision made in yolo mode; the user has not confirmed it. It is listed for the rider's review in Plan 05's SUMMARY; no Phase 6 library file uses the key, so dropping it before Phase 8 would be a core-only change.
- Pad release at chord changes (RESEARCH Pitfall 4; a planner decision made in yolo mode, not heard or accepted by the rider): the core default pad closes its gate at least one beat before every chord change. Its legato is the smaller of 0.9 x chordBars and chordBars - (~ppPadGapBeats / 4), with ~ppPadGapBeats = 1 (1-bar chord 0.75, 2-bar 1.75, 4-bar 3.6), so at 128 BPM the outgoing chord is about 14 dB under its held level when the next chord starts instead of about 6 dB with legato 0.9. D-14 is unchanged (one attack per chord, sounding through the chord). The frozen pad voice's 1.2 s release still fades under the start of the next chord; that remainder is a flagged assumption judged by ear in Plan 05's listening check. Classic keeps its frozen pad generator, so it stays byte-identical to v1.0.
- The post-window line lists the chords as written in the file, one name per chord entry (lift will print `Am F G G`, deep `Am Am F G`), so every shipped progression prints a different line.
- The bass pickup (D-13): among the current chord's tones, the pitch class closest (circular semitone distance) to the next chord's root, the current root counting 0.5 more; that pitch class is played in the octave nearest the next chord's bass root inside MIDI 28-52 (ties to the lower note). This keeps the pickup a chord tone and next to the next root (G to Am plays B1 35, E to Am plays G#1 32, Am to G plays A2 45).
- Pad voicing ties (equal movement) go to the voicing whose mean is nearest the home voicing 45 48 52 (mean 48.333), then to the lower one, so the pad never drifts downward over many cycles.
- `auto` keeps resolving to classic in this plan; Plan 04 adds Auto rotation at the same decision point.
</objective>
