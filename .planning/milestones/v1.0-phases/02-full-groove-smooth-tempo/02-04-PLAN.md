# Phase 2, Plan 04: Full groove (drums, bass, arpeggio, pad) and the phase acceptance listen

## Phase Goal

**As a** rider, **I want to** start, stop and retempo a full trance groove from the app, **so that** tempo changes never jolt my ride.

After this plan the engine plays a full melodic-techno / progressive-trance groove (kick, hat, clap, bass, arpeggio, pad) on the one clock. It glides with every BPM change (Plan 03) and starts and stops cleanly from the app's button (Plans 01 and 02). This closes ENG-01 and runs the phase's end-to-end listening acceptance.

<objective>
Add the clap, arpeggio and pad layers to `supercollider/main.scd` on the shared `~clock` and the existing mix bus. Extend `~ppLayers` to all six layers, and prove in the headless smoke test that every layer plays and sounds. Then prove that Stop cuts a held pad and catches in-flight notes now that a long-sustain voice exists, and run the end-of-phase listening check through the real app.

Purpose: ENG-01 (drums, bass, arpeggio and pad continuously, in style, on one TempoClock). The pad is also the voice that makes "Stop with no hanging notes" (success criterion 4) a real test.
Output: updated `supercollider/main.scd` and `supercollider/smoke_test.scd`.

Working decisions this plan implements (RESEARCH "Gray Areas" groove default; content is reversible and tuned by ear in the listening check):
- Kick, hat and bass stay exactly as Phase 1 built them. Add a clap on beats 2 and 4, a 16th-note arpeggio A3-C4-E4-C4 (MIDI 57, 60, 64, 60), and one pad chord per bar alternating Am (45, 48, 52) and Am with a passing tone (45, 48, 53).
- Fixed loops only. The evolving harmony and variation of GEN-01/02/03 are v2 and out of scope.
- Voices stay as cheap as Phase 1's: at most three oscillators and one filter per voice, and no effects. EPIC-01's filter/drive/reverb mapping is Phase 3 (RESEARCH Pitfall 4).
</objective>
