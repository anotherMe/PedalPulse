
## Some suggestions for an app name

Here are some name ideas that blend music/BPM/beat with cycling/spinning, keeping a clean, techy feel suitable for a SuperCollider-based app:

Direct combinations:

- BeatSpoke – “beat” + “spoke” (of a wheel); suggests rhythm driving the wheel.
- BPM Cadence – straightforward, descriptive; “cadence” is a key cycling metric.
- SpinBeat – simple, energetic, easy to remember.
- CycleBPM – very explicit about what it does.
- PedalPulse – “pedal” for cycling, “pulse” for rhythm/beat.
- RPMusic – plays on RPM (revolutions per minute) + music; very on-point for cycling.
- CadenceFlow – emphasizes smooth BPM changes and riding flow.

More stylized / brandable:

- Velobeat – “velo” (bike) + “beat”; short and catchy.
- Spincycle Audio – a bit longer, but clear and dynamic.
- BeatRPM – mirrors “BPM” but with RPM, tying music and cycling.
- PulsePedal – rhythmic and sporty.
- RevvMusic – “rev” as in engine/cadence + music; energetic vibe.
- Cadentia – invented word from “cadence”; sounds like a product name.
- BeatGear – “gear” evokes both bike gears and musical gear.

If you want something very minimal:

- BPM Velo
- SpinBPM
- BeatCadence
- RPM Beat
